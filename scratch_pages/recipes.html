    Brownies with Fudge Frosting

  • Ingredients
    • 1 box of brownie mix (any brand will do)
    • 3 cups powdered sugar
    • 1/3 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
    • 1/3 cup margarine or butter, softened
    • 2/9 cup boiling water (I usually just put it in the microwave until it bubbles)
    • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Directions
    1. Follow recipe on brownie box, I always use a 9x13 pan but feel free to use your favorite size!
    2. While brownies are cooling, mix powdered sugar and cocoa with a whisk
    3. Add margarine, boiling water, and vailla to your dry mix
    4. Beat together with an electric mixer on low speed until combined
    5. Let frosting cool for 20-30 minutes, then frost the brownies
    6. Enjoy!

    Super Moist Chocolate Cake

  • Cake Ingredients
    • 1 1/2 cups flour
    • 1 1/3 cups sugar
    • 1/2 cups + 2 Tbsp. cocoa powder
    • 1 1/2 tsp. baking soda
    • 1/2 tsp. salt
    • 1/2 cups very hot water
    • 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, melted
    • 2 large eggs, beaten
    • 1 cup buttermilk or lowfat plain yogurt
    • 1 tsp. vanilla
  • Cake Directions
    1. Preheat oven to 350°
    2. Mix flour, sugar, cocoa, baking soda, and salt in a mixing bowl. Whisk together
    3. Whisk in hot water, buter, eggs, and vanilla to dry ingredients
    4. Mix in buttermilk
    5. Line bottom of 8 or 9 in spring-form pan with was paper, or butter/spray the pan
    6. Pour batter into pan, spreading it evenly
    7. Bake for 40 minutes
    8. Let cake cool in pan for 10 minutes, then take it out of the pan to cool the rest of the way
  • Ganache Ingredients
    • 6 ounces semisweet chocolate chips
    • 6 tbsp. butter
  • Ganache Directions
    1. Put chocolate chips and butter in microwave safe bowl/container
    2. Heat in 30 second increments, mixing the ingredients between each round, until fully combined
    3. Pour ganache on completely cooled cake
    4. Enjoy!
